---

Growers have the data, but not the expertise, time, or resources to act on it.
Aiterra builds **Fieldhand AI** to close that gap.

**Farm data in. Zones, soil test orders, and prescriptions out.** Fieldhand turns
field and satellite data into zone maps and variable-rate prescriptions, lets
growers order soil sampling with one tap, and answers agronomy questions in plain
language. Every number is explainable and traceable to its source: the lab,
method and date, or clearly labeled as modeled.

- **Live:** web app for growers and the advisors who work with them
- **In testing:** mobile app with offline field capture
- **Building:** results from labs and servicers that publish back automatically, so each new lab result sharpens the next recommendation

→ [aiterra.ag](https://aiterra.ag)
